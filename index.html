Research & Development Initiatives (Click to Review Technical Abstract)

01. Indigenous Flight Controller & ESC Firmware +

Custom in-house STM32-based autopilot hardware and motor electronic speed controller (ESC) firmware with active braking and real-time current logging, reducing dependency on proprietary foreign flight stacks.

02. Collaborative Defense Drone Project (DRDO Track) +

Classified unmanned aerial platform developed in technical consultation with DRDO researchers. Focuses on low radar-cross-section composites, redundant power rails, and long-endurance loiter aerodynamics.

03. GPS-Denied Navigation & Optical Waypoint Guidance +

Stereo vision, optical flow, and inertial sensor fusion allowing micro-UAVs to maintain steady localization and obstacle avoidance in indoor or electronically contested environments.
