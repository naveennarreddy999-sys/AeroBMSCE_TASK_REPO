Who We Are — Specialized Engineering Wings

Collaborating with students across 10+ college engineering departments. Our multi-disciplinary teams take aerial concepts from computational CFD modeling to real-world flight lines.

WING 01 GLOBAL

Team Yaksha

Flagship division competing in the international SAE Aero Design Series in North America. Specializes in maximizing payload lift fraction within strict box dimensions.

Circuit: SAE Aero Design West (Texas, USA)

Accolade: 2nd Globally (MATLAB Trophy)

WING 02 AEROMODELLING

Team Blue Impulse

Classical aeromodelling division engineering fixed-wing high-lift monoplanes. Experts in high-camber balsa profiling, laser-cut ribs, and short-takeoff payload aircraft.

Focus: High Cargo Fractions & STOL

Track: National Aeromodelling Podiums

WING 03 AUTONOMOUS

Team Flying Phantoms

Autonomous drone and hybrid VTOL engineering division. Develops computer vision tracking, optical flow indoor hovering, and precision servo-actuated payload drops.

Focus: ROS, OpenCV, Pixhawk Autopilot

Arena: ISRO IROC & National Drone Fests

WING 04 FPV RACING

Team Vectors

Extreme speed and drone racing division. Constructs sub-250g carbon-fiber racing frames with custom PID tuning capable of speeds exceeding 140 km/h.

Focus: High-C LiPo Chemistry & 5.8GHz VTX

Arena: NITK Drone Racing Challenge

SAE MICRO CLASS
DESIGNATION: AEROBMSCE-MC-26 FLIGHT QUALIFIED

Micro Class Aeromodel

Engineered strictly within modular transport envelope constraints. Prioritizes low structural empty weight, carbon-fiber pultruded spar reinforcement, and rapid cold assembly under competition time pressure.

Propulsion: Brushless Outrunner + APC 8x4E Prop
Payload Capacity: 1.8x Airframe Empty Weight
Structural Materials: 3K Carbon Tube + Selected Balsa Ribs
Assembly Benchmark: < 120 Seconds Rapid Assembly

Research & Development Initiatives

100% of our flight systems research, technical fabrication, and testing is supported through the institutional R&D department at BMS College of Engineering.

AVIONICS

Indigenous Flight Controller

End-to-end hardware schematic and STM32 firmware design for real-time sensor processing and motor telemetry without proprietary software dependencies.

STATUS: BENCHTOP VALIDATED
PROPULSION

Electronic Speed Controller (ESC)

High-efficiency brushless ESC electronics capable of regenerative braking, current spike mitigation, and sustained high amp discharge.

STATUS: PROTOTYPE TESTING
AUTONOMY

GPS-Denied Navigation Systems

Visual inertial odometry (VIO) and optical flow sensor fusion for autonomous waypoint navigation in electronically contested environments.

STATUS: INDOOR FLIGHT TESTING
FIXED-WING

Multi-Mission Surveillance UAV

Reconfigurable airframe with convertible propulsion pods and an integrated modular quick-disconnect sensor bay for thermal aerial reconnaissance.

STATUS: FLIGHT READY
DEFENSE TRACK RESTRICTED

Confidential Defense Project

Collaborative unmanned aerial platform developed in technical association with DRDO researchers (specific aerodynamic parameters classified).

COLLABORATION: DRDO
AERODYNAMICS

Morphing Wingtip Mechanisms

Adaptive spanwise geometry that actively reshapes during cruise to suppress induced wingtip vortices and minimize drag profiles.

STATUS: CFD VALIDATION
CONTROL

Deployable Directional Micro-Fins
